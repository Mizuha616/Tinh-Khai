- Correct classification: "thảm họa Lumiel cấp SSS".
- Cy-tech detected an unidentified SSS-class entity in the northern sea. Deviant departed urgently at noon. The encounter itself remains undisclosed.
- Aeva learned about the mission in the afternoon and waited anxiously overnight.
- Deviant returned at dawn uninjured. His white hoodie was covered in blood that was not his. Blood source is undisclosed.
- Emotional symmetry: Deviant was reluctant to release Aeva in the morning before departure; Aeva held onto him after his return.
- Deviant promised to inform Aeva before future departures.
- Modern narration uses "cô/cô ấy" for Aeva, not "nàng". Couple's direct speech uses "anh/em".
- Aeva's boundary was "Hôm nay chỉ hôn thôi"; Deviant respected it.
- Speculative extraterrestrial origins and Lumirith biology are not established by this chapter.
